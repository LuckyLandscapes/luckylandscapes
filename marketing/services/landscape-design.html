Design That Fits Your Use

Drainage & Grading

Lincoln's heavy clay sheds water instead of soaking it up, so grade is usually the make-or-break detail. We move water away from the foundation, cut in swales or rock runs where it pools, and set elevations before anything pretty goes on top. Solve the water and everything else lasts.

Hardscape Backbone

Paver patios, walkways, and retaining walls built on a deep, properly compacted base so they don't heave through a Nebraska freeze-thaw. The hardscape is the bones of the whole design — it's the work we're known for, and it anchors everything we plant around it.

Planting & Beds

Trees, shrubs, perennials, and beds chosen for our climate and placed where they'll thrive — mulch on the planted, hot-exposure beds and rock only where heat and dryness don't matter. We think about what the yard looks like in year three, not just on install day.

FAQ

Landscape Design
Questions Answered

What does design-and-build actually mean?

It means the same company that draws the plan also builds it — no handing your design off to a separate contractor who didn't draw it and wasn't there for the conversation. We handle the whole arc: consultation, layout and plant plan, demolition, grading and drainage, hardscape, planting, mulch, and final cleanup. One crew owns the result start to finish, which is why the finished yard actually matches what you agreed to. For the look most Lincoln homeowners are after these days, see our modern landscape design ideas for Lincoln.

How much does a full landscape design cost in Lincoln, NE?

It scales with property size, how much hardscape is involved, and plant material. A front-yard refresh often lands around $3,000–$5,000, while a full backyard transformation with patios, walls, grading, and plantings can run from $10,000 well into the $50,000+ range. We do free consultations and hand you a detailed, itemized quote, so you see exactly where the money goes before committing to anything. Get your free quote.

How long does a landscape project take from start to finish?

A front-yard refresh might be 3–5 days; a full backyard build with pavers, retaining walls, and plantings typically runs 2–4 weeks. Weather, material lead times, and the amount of grading move that around. You get a real schedule at the consultation, and because it's one crew rather than a chain of subcontractors waiting on each other, the timeline doesn't stall between phases.

Can you fix the drainage and grading problems on my clay lot?

Yes — and on Lincoln's heavy clay it's usually the part that matters most. Clay sheds water instead of absorbing it, so a pretty design that ignores grade ends up with a soggy lawn, water against the foundation, or a patio that heaves. We re-grade to move water where it belongs, build in swales or rock drainage runs where needed, and set hardscape on a properly compacted base so it survives the freeze-thaw. Solving the water is what makes the rest last.

Do you handle HOA approvals?

Yes. We provide the detailed plans and material lists you submit to your HOA for approval — plus a 3D rendering on request — and we design within the guidelines from the start so you're not redrawing after a rejection. Plenty of our clients are in covenant-controlled neighborhoods around Lincoln, so this is routine — we'll tell you up front if something you want is likely to get flagged.

Do I need to be home during the project?

Not for most of it. We send progress by text and photos so you can follow along from work or out of town — we just need access to the work area, a water spigot, and any gates. We coordinate the start and the walk-through with you, and the day-to-day in between runs without you having to take time off.
