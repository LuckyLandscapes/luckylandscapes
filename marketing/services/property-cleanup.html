FAQ

Cleanup
Questions Answered

What items do you haul away?

Yard waste, brush, branches, old furniture, appliances, construction debris, and general junk all go in our dump trailer. If it fits and it's not hazardous, we'll take it. What we can't haul: paint, chemicals, fuels, and certain electronics — those have special disposal rules. If you're not sure about something, send a photo when you request a quote and we'll tell you straight.

How much can you haul in one trip?

We run a 6x12 dump trailer rated to about 12,000 lbs, so a surprising amount of brush, debris, or junk goes in a single load — far more than a pickup bed or a few trips to the curb. Bigger cleanouts just mean more loads to the transfer station; we'll estimate how many it'll take when we see the pile and roll that into one flat price.

How do you price a property cleanup?

Flat rate per job, not by the hour, so you know the number before we start and there's no meter running while we work. The price reflects the labor to load it, how many dump-trailer loads it takes, and the dump fee — figure roughly $100 per load at the transfer station. You approve a fixed quote up front; that's the price.

How quickly can you schedule a cleanup in Lincoln, NE?

Most cleanup jobs get scheduled within the same week, and we can often do next-day for urgent situations — a property showing, a code-enforcement letter, a move-out deadline. Cleanups are also some of the easiest work to slot between bigger projects, so tell us your deadline when you request a quote and we'll usually hit it.

Do you offer recurring cleanup services?

Yes. Beyond one-time cleanouts we set up recurring service for rental turnovers, commercial lots, and anyone who'd rather not let seasonal debris pile up — spring brush, fall leaves, storm drop. We'll build a schedule around how fast your property actually accumulates, so it's handled before it becomes a weekend you dread. Get a free quote to get on the schedule.
