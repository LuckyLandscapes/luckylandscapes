# Google Business Profile & Local SEO Playbook

> Created 2026-06-14. This is a **do-this-list for Riley**, not background context. The website is already well-optimized (schema, area pages, blog, conversion). The local-pack slump is an **off-site GBP problem**, and the levers below are what actually move it. Work the top section first.

## The situation (why rank slipped)

Google reclassified Lucky from a **storefront** to a **service-area business (SAB)** after an audit. SAB listings rank by *proximity to the searcher* far more than storefronts do — so the old "storefront pin" advantage is gone, and we dropped from ~3rd to ~5th in the local pack. We can't change distance. We **can** win on the other two ranking factors: **relevance** (categories, services, posts, website) and **prominence** (reviews, photos, citations, activity). Luxury Landscapes is beating us on SEO mostly on **prominence** — they have more reviews and a more active profile. That's catchable.

**The three levers that actually move SAB local rank, in order of ROI:**
1. **Review velocity** (#1 controllable factor) — steady new reviews, not a stale pile.
2. **Weekly GBP posts** — signals an active business + adds keyword relevance.
3. **Fresh geo-relevant photos** — every job, from the phone.

Everything below operationalizes those three. None of it costs money — it costs 15 minutes a week.

---

## 1. Review engine (do this first — highest ROI)

**Target: 2–4 new Google reviews per week, every week.** Velocity matters more than total. A profile gaining 3 reviews/week outranks a stale one with more total reviews. Luxury Landscapes is beatable on velocity if we're consistent.

**The system (already half-built in luckyapp):**
- luckyapp's **Review Request card** is on every completed job + customer page (Jobs → [job] → green card once complete). It opens *your own* Messages/email so the ask comes from your number — that converts ~3× better than an automated blast.
- **Set the review link once:** luckyapp → Settings → Company Profile → "Use my Lucky Landscapes link." Until that's set, the buttons don't work.

**The discipline:** ask **every** happy customer, the **day the job finishes**, while they're standing in the new yard. Same-day asks convert far better than "I'll text them later." Texts from your phone beat email ~2:1 — lead with text.

**Text template (paste from the Review card, then personalize one line):**
> Hey [First name] — Riley with Lucky Landscapes. It was great getting your [project] done this week. If you were happy with it, a quick Google review means the world to a young local crew like ours and helps other Lincoln folks find us. Takes 30 seconds: [review link]. Thank you!

**Email template (for customers who prefer email):**
> Subject: Quick favor, [First name]?
>
> Hi [First name],
>
> Thanks again for trusting us with your [project]. We're a small, local Lincoln crew and Google reviews are how most of our new customers find us. If you've got 30 seconds, we'd be grateful for an honest review: [review link].
>
> Either way, thank you — call or text me anytime at (402) 405-5475.
> — Riley, Lucky Landscapes

**Respond to every review within 48 hours** (responding is itself a ranking signal and shows prospects you're engaged). Keep it short, warm, and work the city/service in naturally:

- *5-star:* "Thank you, [Name]! It was a pleasure building your [paver patio] in [neighborhood]. Enjoy it this summer — call us anytime."
- *4-star:* "Thanks [Name]! Glad you're happy with the [project]. If there's anything that would've made it a 5, tell me — we want it right. — Riley"
- *Critical (stay calm, never argue):* "Thanks for the honest feedback, [Name]. This isn't the experience we want anyone to have. I'd like to make it right — please call me directly at (402) 405-5475. — Riley"

**Never** buy reviews or ask in bulk gift-card style — Google filters obvious solicitation and can suspend the profile. Genuine same-day asks are the play.

## 2. Weekly GBP posts (15 min/week)

Post **once a week, minimum.** Google Posts expire visually after ~7 days, so cadence > polish. Each post: 1 photo (from a real job), 2–4 sentences working in a service + "Lincoln," and a **Call** or **Book** button pointing to the quote page or phone.

Rotate these themes so it never feels repetitive: **(a)** recent project + photo, **(b)** a tip (pull from the blog), **(c)** a seasonal reminder, **(d)** a service spotlight, **(e)** an offer/availability nudge. Ten ready-to-paste posts below — change the bracketed bits, attach a job photo, set the CTA, post.

1. **(Project)** "Wrapped a paver patio in [neighborhood] this week — base dug deep and compacted so it'll stay flat through Nebraska's freeze-thaw winters. Thinking about a patio before fall? We do free in-person quotes across Lincoln. 🍀" → *Button: Get a quote*
2. **(Tip)** "Lincoln lawn tip: never cut more than ⅓ of the blade in one mow, and keep cool-season grass at 3.5″+. Scalping it in June = a thin, stressed lawn in July. Need it handled? We mow across Lincoln. 🍀" → *Button: Call now*
3. **(Seasonal)** "Mid-June is prime time for a mulch refresh — it locks in moisture before the July heat and makes the whole yard look finished. We install mulch and rock beds all over Lincoln. Free quote in about 2 minutes." → *Button: Get a quote*
4. **(Service)** "Retaining wall leaning or a slope washing out? We build engineered retaining walls across Lincoln that handle our clay soil and grade. Free on-site estimate, fixed price, fully insured. 🍀" → *Button: Get a quote*
5. **(Project)** "Before & after on a full bed redesign in [neighborhood] — tired junipers out, clean edges and fresh plantings in. Same crew start to finish. Want yours done right? Lincoln's local crew is a text away: (402) 405-5475." → *Button: Call now*
6. **(Tip)** "Pavers vs. concrete in Nebraska? Concrete cracks with our freeze-thaw; a paver patio flexes with the ground and repairs invisibly. Full breakdown on our blog. We install both — happy to walk your yard." → *Button: Learn more (link the blog post)*
7. **(Offer/availability)** "We've got room on the schedule for summer hardscape and cleanup projects in Lincoln. Free, fixed-price, in-person quotes — no pressure, no hidden fees. Call or text Riley at (402) 405-5475. 🍀" → *Button: Call now*
8. **(Service)** "New build with a thin builder lawn? We turn builder-grade yards in [Pine Lake / Stevens Creek / The Highlands] into real landscapes — soil work, shaping, planting. Free quote." → *Button: Get a quote*
9. **(Seasonal)** "Fire pit season is the longest season — March through November in Lincoln. We build prefab and custom stone fire pits, often paired with a patio to share the base. Free design quote." → *Button: Get a quote*
10. **(Project + proof)** "Another 5-star finish in Lincoln this week. We're a young, local, owner-run crew — Riley does every quote in person and the same crew builds the job. See why folks trust us: [review link or /gallery]." → *Button: Get a quote*

**Pro move:** add the city/neighborhood name to most posts. SAB local relevance is built one geo-mention at a time.

## 3. Photos (every job, from the phone)

Photos are a top-5 GBP engagement factor and prospects judge you on them. **Upload 3–5 new photos per week minimum** — Google rewards profiles that add media steadily.

- **Every completed job:** 2–3 wide "after" shots in good light + 1 detail shot. Before/afters perform best.
- **Shoot landscape (horizontal),** good light (morning/evening), straighten the horizon. Riley already uploads to luckyapp's Marketing Gallery from the field — also drop the best ones straight onto the **GBP** profile (GBP photos and website gallery are separate surfaces; feed both).
- **Name the files before upload** where possible: `paver-patio-lincoln-ne.jpg`, not `IMG_4821.jpg`. Minor signal, free to do.
- Add a few **team/truck** photos (builds trust) and keep the **logo + cover** current.

## 4. GBP profile optimization (one-time, ~30 min)

Knock these out once; they raise relevance permanently.

**Primary category:** `Landscaper`
**Secondary categories (add all that apply — they expand which searches you show for):** `Landscape designer`, `Lawn care service`, `Paving contractor` (for paver patios), `Retaining wall supplier` or `Masonry contractor`, `Fence contractor`, `Snow removal service` *(only if you actually offer it — Macoy's idea; skip if Riley vetoes)*, `Garbage collection / Debris removal service` (for cleanups).

**Business description (paste-ready, ~720 chars — GBP allows 750):**
> Lucky Landscapes is a local, owner-run landscaping and hardscaping company serving Lincoln, NE and the surrounding towns. We build paver patios, retaining walls, walkways, and fire pits; install garden beds, mulch, and plantings; handle lawn care, fencing, and property cleanups; and design full landscape transformations. Owner Riley Kopf does every quote in person, and the same crew that quotes your project is the one that builds it. Fixed-price quotes with no hidden fees, fully insured, and we're not done until you're happy. Free on-site estimates across Lincoln, Waverly, Hickman, Seward, and Lancaster County — call or text (402) 405-5475.

**Services (add each with a 1-line description — these are keyword-relevant surfaces):** Lawn Care & Mowing, Garden Beds & Mulch, Paver Patios, Retaining Walls, Walkways, Fire Pits, Fence Installation, Property Cleanup & Hauling, Landscape Design & Build. Use the blog/service-page copy for descriptions.

**Service area:** define it as Lincoln + Waverly, Hickman, Seward, Bennet, Roca, Davey, Emerald (and Lancaster County). Matches the website's `areaServed`. Keep it tight — listing far-flung cities you don't really serve dilutes proximity relevance.

**Attributes to turn on:** "Online estimates," "Onsite services," "Identifies as veteran-/family-/locally-owned" as applicable, "Free estimates," appointment/quote link.

**Q&A seed (post these yourself from a second Google account, then answer from the business — owner-seeded Q&A is allowed and controls the narrative):**
- Q: "Do you do free estimates in Lincoln?" → A: "Yes — every quote is free and done in person, usually within a day or two. Call or text (402) 405-5475."
- Q: "Are you insured?" → A: "Fully insured on every job, from the first day to the last."
- Q: "What areas do you serve?" → A: "Lincoln and the surrounding towns — Waverly, Hickman, Seward, and most of Lancaster County."
- Q: "Do you build paver patios and retaining walls?" → A: "Yes, hardscaping is one of our specialties — patios, walls, walkways, and fire pits, built for Nebraska's freeze-thaw."

## 5. NAP & citation consistency (prominence)

Google cross-checks your Name / Address-area / Phone across the web. Inconsistency hurts. Make every listing read **exactly**: `Lucky Landscapes` · service-area: `Lincoln, NE` · `(402) 405-5475` · `https://luckylandscapes.com`.

- Claim/keep consistent: GBP, Bing Places, Apple Business Connect, Facebook, Instagram, Yelp, Nextdoor, Angi, Thumbtack, BBB.
- **Nextdoor** is high-leverage for Lincoln neighborhoods specifically — neighbor recommendations there feed word-of-mouth and the brand searches that lift local rank.
- Don't publish a street address anywhere if the home base shouldn't be public — keep it a service-area listing everywhere (matches the SAB decision and the website schema).

---

## 6. Measure it (monthly, 10 min)

- **GBP Insights / Performance:** track *calls*, *direction requests* (less relevant for SAB), *website clicks*, and *searches* (discovery vs. direct). Rising calls = it's working.
- **Review count + velocity:** are we adding 2–4/week? If not, the asks aren't happening — that's the fix, not anything fancier.
- **Local rank:** use the luckyapp `/local-rank` geo-grid checker (already built) to watch pin rank for "landscaper near me," "paver patio Lincoln," etc. across the grid. Re-run monthly; expect movement to lag review velocity by 4–8 weeks.
- **The honest expectation:** SAB local rank responds slowly. Consistent reviews + posts + photos for 6–8 weeks moves it; one big burst then silence does not. This is a cadence game.

### TL;DR for a busy week
1. Text every finished customer for a review **today** (from the luckyapp card).
2. Post once to GBP with a job photo (pick one from §2).
3. Upload this week's job photos to GBP.

That's the whole engine. 15 minutes, every week, beats any one-time website change for local rank.
