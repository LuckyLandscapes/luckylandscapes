#### Above-ground pool base prep (sub-service of hardscaping)
- **Scope:** Excavation + grade level to 1/2" tolerance, 2" compacted screenings base, paver/concrete sand top layer, optional 16x16 patio block pads under each leg.
- **Typical job size:** half-day to full-day. Pool sizes 12'–24' round, 12x24 oval. Pad area = pool diameter + 2 ft.
- **Materials:** 1.5–2 yd screenings (Outdoor Solutions Roca / Stewart Sand / Frontier Stone, ~$35–45/yd), concrete sand (NOT mason sand — see blog post), 6–8 patio blocks at ~$3 each.
- **Pricing:** TODO — likely $500–$1,500 standalone depending on slope + access. Bundle with paver walkway around pool for higher ticket.
- **Seasonality:** April–July peak (pool delivery season). Book quotes in April for May–June installs.
- **Common customer mistake we fix:** they bought play sand or mason sand, ground isn't level, pool fails year 1.

## Pricing principles
- **Hourly labor rate (loaded):** $60/hr per person
- **Quote formula:** `(labor + materials) × 1.30 + dump fees + delivery fees`
  - **30% profit margin** is applied to **labor + materials only**.
  - **Dump fees** ($100) and **delivery fees** ($2.50/mi, $50 minimum) are passthrough — **no margin** added.
  - Materials are billed at **cost** before margin is applied. The 30% is the markup.
